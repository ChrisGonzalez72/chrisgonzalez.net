# On Record — Support

On Record writes up your meetings on your own Mac: a summary, the decisions,
action items with owners and due dates, and a transcript with who said what.
Every point links back to the moment it was said.

**Contact:** [chris@chrisgonzalez.net](mailto:chris@chrisgonzalez.net). We
usually reply within two business days.
Privacy: [Privacy Policy](/apps/on-record/privacy/)

## What you need

- **A Mac with Apple silicon** running On Record. It does all the
  transcription and writing.
- **iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch** are optional: they record and show the
  results. They need On Record on your Mac, signed in to the same iCloud
  account. One purchase covers every device.
- Made for meetings in English.

## Getting started

1. Install On Record on your Mac, open **Settings → Models** and download a
   language model (Qwen3 8B is a 4.6 GB download). Transcription also works
   with Apple Speech without a download.
2. Press **Record** during a meeting, or bring in an audio file with
   **Import Audio** (⌘O) on Home or Meetings, or drop it on the Meetings list.
3. Open the meeting for its Summary, Notes, Actions, Decisions and Transcript.
   Click a time to hear the moment it came from.
4. On iPhone or iPad, sign in to the same iCloud account as your Mac and tap
   Record. The recording goes to your Mac and the results come back.

## Common questions

**My iPhone recording hasn't been processed.**
Check that On Record is installed and open on your Mac, that both devices use
the same iCloud account (or that you've shared with your Mac in iPhone
**Settings → Transfer**), and that **Send recordings to my Mac** is on. Pull
down on the Meetings list to check now.

**Summaries, notes or actions are missing.**
They need a language model. On the Mac, open **Settings → Models** and
download one.

**The Mac doesn't record the call audio.**
Allow On Record to record system audio when macOS asks, or in **System
Settings → Privacy & Security**.

**Who said what is wrong.**
Open the meeting, choose **Who is speaking?** and name the speakers. Named
voices are suggested in later meetings.

**A word or name is transcribed wrong.**
Correct the line in the transcript (the original is always kept), or add the
word to your vocabulary so it's spelled right next time.

**Does my meeting go to the cloud?**
Your meetings are processed on your Mac and never sent to an AI service.
Recordings and results move between your own devices through your iCloud
account. See the [Privacy Policy](/apps/on-record/privacy/).

**How do I delete a meeting?**
On the Mac, open it and choose **Delete Meeting…** from its menu. On iPhone
or iPad, use **Delete Everywhere**.

## Sending diagnostics

The diagnostics log lists what the app did with error codes only, never
meeting content. To send it: on the Mac, **Help → Diagnostics Log**; on
iPhone or iPad, **Settings → Diagnostics Log**, then share it by email.
