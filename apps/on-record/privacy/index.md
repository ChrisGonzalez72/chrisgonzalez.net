# On Record — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

On Record ("the app") is made by Christopher Michael Gonzalez ("we"). This
policy covers On Record for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

## The short version

We don't collect any data from the app. We have no On Record servers, no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your recordings, their
transcripts and everything written from them stay on your devices and in your
own iCloud account, which we cannot access.

## What the app does with your meetings

- **Recording.** The app records audio from your microphone, and on the Mac
  optionally the audio another app plays (for example a Zoom or Teams call),
  only when you start a recording.
- **Processing.** Transcription, speaker detection, summaries, decisions,
  action items and answers to your questions are made on your Mac, by models
  that run on the Mac. Your meeting content is never sent to an AI service,
  to us, or to anyone else.
- **Moving between your devices.** Recordings made on iPhone, iPad or Apple
  Watch reach your Mac, and the results come back, through your own iCloud
  account (Apple's iCloud, in your private storage, or in a share you set up
  with another Apple Account you choose). Meetings recorded on the Mac are
  also kept in your iCloud so your iPhone can show them. Apple handles this
  storage under its own privacy policy; we have no access to it.
- **Voice profiles.** When you name speakers, the voice information used to
  recognise them stays on your Mac.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Microphone:** to record meetings you choose to record.
- **System audio (Mac):** to record another app's call audio together with
  your microphone, when you turn it on.
- **Speech Recognition:** to turn a question you speak to Ask into text, on
  your device. The audio isn't sent anywhere or kept.
- **Calendars (Mac, optional):** to name a recording after the calendar event
  happening when you press Record. Only the event's name is used, and it stays
  on your devices.

## Network connections

- **Model downloads.** When you choose to download a transcription or language
  model, the Mac downloads it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). These
  downloads contain no meeting content. As with any download, Hugging Face can
  see your IP address; see its privacy policy.
- **iCloud.** Used only for moving your own recordings and results between
  your devices, as described above.

The app makes no other network connections.

## Diagnostics

The app keeps a diagnostics log on each device listing what it did (for
example "sync finished") with error codes only, never meeting content, titles,
names or file paths. It stays on the device unless you choose to share it
with us.

## Sharing and exporting

If you share or export a meeting (for example as a PDF), it goes only where
you send it.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a meeting (Delete Everywhere on iPhone or iPad, Delete on the Mac)
removes it from your devices and from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data it keeps on that device; to remove
data from iCloud, delete the meetings first or manage your iCloud storage in
your device's Settings.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
