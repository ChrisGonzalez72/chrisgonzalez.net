# On Record

**Private meeting notes, made on your Mac.**

On Record listens to your meetings and writes them up: a clear summary, the
decisions that were made, the action items with owners and due dates, and a
full transcript with who said what. Every point links back to the exact moment
it was said, so you can check it with one tap.

All of it is made on your own Mac. No On Record servers, no account, no meeting
bot joining your calls.

## Private by design

- Transcription, speaker detection and AI writing run on your Mac, using
  models you download once.
- Your recordings and results never touch an On Record server.
- iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch recordings reach your Mac through your own
  iCloud, and the results come back the same way.
- Your meetings are never used for advertising, tracking or AI training.

## Record anywhere

- **Mac:** record your microphone together with the call audio from Zoom,
  Teams, Webex or any other app, or import audio files you already have.
- **iPhone:** start in one tap from the app, a Home Screen widget, Control
  Center, the Action button or Siri. Recording keeps going on the Lock Screen
  with a Live Activity.
- **iPad:** record in the app, even offline; recordings go to your Mac when
  the iPad is back online.
- **Apple Watch:** start, pause and stop from your wrist.

## Everything written up for you

- Summary and detailed notes, organized by topic
- Decisions, kept apart from discussion
- Action items with owners and due dates
- A full transcript, split by speaker
- **Ask** a question across your meetings and get an answer with links to the
  moments it came from

## What you need

A Mac with Apple silicon does the processing. iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch
record and show the results; they need On Record on your Mac, signed in to the
same iCloud account. One purchase covers every device. Made for meetings in
English.
